Here are my thoughts on the Scope of Work Contract:

-   Scale and increments seem reasonable

	-   Consider a "Contact Us" selection if districts want more than 4 classes
	-   With respect to feasibility:

		-   I'm not sure what kind of funding structures and vehicles we have in place for a program like this at the school level. I have ideas about how this would work but would need to talk details with my principal.

		-   On the surface, I don't know if this would be able to run for the 2025-26 school year as budgets have already been approved. The next year's budget is typically tabled around April and May of the previous year.

		-   I would explore options to see if this could be funded at the board level.

-   Learning experiences

	-   Including assessment pieces and specific alignments with provincial curriculum would be important for future iterations.
	-   Otherwise this seems complete.
