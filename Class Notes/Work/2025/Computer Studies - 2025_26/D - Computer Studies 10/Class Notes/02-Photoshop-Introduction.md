##  Creating a New Photoshop File

It's important to know what screen/media you are targeting so that we can set the **right** canvas size.

* Are you printing out the final product?
	* How big do you want to print it?
* Is your final product going on a screen?
	* What kind of screen is this going to be viewed in?

Do this in Photoshop:
* Click the blue new file button
* We'll generally use three different **size dimensions** in this class:
	* **Print material**
		* 4 inches by 6 inches
	* **Screen**
		* 1920 pixels by 1080 pixels
		* 3840 pixels by 2160 pixels

* We will also generally use only one **resolution**:
	* 300 pixels per inch

## Layer

Layers allow us to group our ideas together in ways that make sense.

Layers are organized in a **stack**. Layers at the top of the list are on top of the **stack**. If they're at the bottom, they will appear below the other layers.
