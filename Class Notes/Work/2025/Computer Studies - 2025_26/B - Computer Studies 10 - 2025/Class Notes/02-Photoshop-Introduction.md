##  Creating a New Photoshop File

It's important to know what screen/media you are targeting so that we can set the **right** canvas size.

* Are you printing out the final product?
* Is your final product going on a screen?

* Click the blue new file button
* We'll generally use three different **size dimensions** in this class:
	* **Printing our images**
		* 4 inches by 6 inches
	* **Viewing on a screen**
		* 1920 pixels by 1080 pixels (1080p)
		* 3940 pixels by 2160 pixels (4K)

* We will also generally use one **resolution**:
	* 300 pixels per inch
